# MSME 45-day payment rule (MSMED Act s.15; Income-tax s.43B(h))
Status: **UNVERIFIED**  | Reviewer: team member plus CA before the app shows it

- Claim to verify: a buyer must pay a micro/small enterprise supplier within the agreed period, not exceeding 45 days from the day of acceptance (MSMED Act 2006 s.15; "appointed day" s.2(b)). Income-tax Act 1961 s.43B(h) allows the buyer's deduction only on actual payment within that period. The equivalent in the Income-tax Act 2025 is unknown. Conditional on the vendor holding Udyam registration and on a written agreement. **[UNVERIFIED]**
