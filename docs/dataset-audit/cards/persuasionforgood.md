## Domain fit
50 sampled (seed 42), read [COMPUTED].
- Domain: persuading a stranger to donate part of a task payment to Save the Children. Roles: persuader/persuadee (asymmetric, like freelancer pitching), but the persuadee bargains about **whether** to give, not price.
- Amount pattern 4/50 sample, 6.66% full pass; amounts are tiny ($0–$2) [COMPUTED]. Firm 0.37%, soft 14.82% [COMPUTED]. Register: polite, informal English.
- Main mismatch: charity persuasion, not a commercial rate; no counter-offers.
- Good: "Would you consider donating $.60?" (ANCHOR); "You can donate from $0 to $2 of your payment. Is that doable?" (range anchor); "this is my full time job and i depend on every penny from it. So no" (REJECT + BUDGET objection).
- Worst: a long personal hardship disclosure (not quoted, sensitive); a pasted system instruction about the donation form; "around $1500 per month" (out of context; quoted text, not a measured value).
